Hello! I'm **Kouyakou-Abalo SIMSOBA**, a **Google DeepMind Scholar** and MSc candidate in **Artificial Intelligence for Science** at Stellenbosch University through AIMS South Africa. I hold a Master's in **Mathematics (Statistics)** from PAUSTI as an **African Union Scholar**, and a Bachelor's in Mathematics & Statistics from **Université de Kara, Togo**.

My research sits at the intersection of **deep learning** and **medical imaging** — histopathology, MRI, multi-magnification fusion, uncertainty quantification, and model explainability. I care about building AI systems that are not just accurate but **reliable and interpretable** in clinical settings. I am currently extending my MSc work toward multimodal learning and generative augmentation for limited labelled data, and I am actively seeking a fully funded **Ph.D. position** in machine learning, statistics, or trustworthy AI.
