<p class="lede">I am <strong>Kouyakou-Abalo SIMSOBA</strong>, a mathematician and AI researcher from Togo. I hold two Master's degrees — an <strong>M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence for Science</strong> from AIMS South Africa and Stellenbosch University, where I was a <strong>Google DeepMind Scholar</strong>, and an <strong>M.Sc. in Mathematics (Statistics)</strong> from PAUSTI and JKUAT in Kenya, where I was an <strong>African Union Scholar</strong>. I now live and work in Lomé, where I also lecture at ESAM.</p>

<p class="lede">My research focuses on making deep learning models reliable in medical settings — particularly histopathology and medical imaging. I am currently looking for a <strong>fully funded Ph.D. position starting in 2027</strong>.</p>

<div class="section-divider"><span>Research</span></div>

<div class="kv">
<div class="kv-row"><div class="kv-label">Trustworthy medical AI</div><div class="kv-val">My M.Sc. thesis produced <strong>MultiMagNet</strong>, a four-stream ResNet-50 fusion architecture for breast cancer histopathology that reaches <strong>AUC 0.935</strong> and remains robust when magnification levels are missing at inference. Beyond accuracy, I assess calibration, uncertainty, and out-of-distribution behaviour — and I study when a model should defer to a human expert. I am currently extending this toward multimodal fusion of images and clinical records.</div></div>
<div class="kv-row"><div class="kv-label">Robust learning &amp; forecasting</div><div class="kv-val">My first paper introduced a <strong>differentiable Minkowski–log-cosh loss</strong> for LSTM-based time-series forecasting, reducing error by 13% on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data while limiting the impact of outliers.</div></div>
<div class="kv-row"><div class="kv-label">Computational biology</div><div class="kv-val">At the MMED clinic (ICI3D / SACEMA, Stellenbosch) I worked on compartmental and stochastic models of Mpox transmission in South Kivu, DRC — SIR, SEIR, and SEIRV formulations with parameter estimation from real surveillance data.</div></div>
</div>
