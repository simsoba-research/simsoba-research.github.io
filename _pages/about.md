<p class="lede">Hello! I'm <strong>Kouyakou-Abalo SIMSOBA</strong>, a <strong>Google DeepMind Scholar</strong> and holder of two Master's degrees: an <strong>M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence for Science</strong> (Stellenbosch University / AIMS South Africa) and an <strong>M.Sc. in Mathematics, Statistics</strong> (PAUSTI / JKUAT, as an <strong>African Union Scholar</strong>). I am originally from Togo and currently based in Lomé, where I teach at ESAM.</p>

<p class="lede">My research sits at the intersection of <strong>deep learning</strong> and <strong>medical image analysis</strong>, with a focus on models that are robust, well-calibrated and trustworthy by design. My M.Sc. thesis introduced <strong>MultiMagNet</strong>, a four-stream CNN fusion architecture for breast cancer histopathology (<strong>AUC 0.935</strong>), now under review at <strong>ICLR 2027</strong>. My earlier work produced a <strong>differentiable Minkowski–Log-Cosh loss</strong> for robust LSTM forecasting, published in <strong>IEEE Access</strong>. I work in <strong>Python, PyTorch and TensorFlow</strong> with GPU/HPC training experience, and I am an invited speaker at <strong>ACIAM 2026</strong>. I am available for a <strong>fully funded Ph.D. starting in 2027</strong> in machine learning, statistics or trustworthy AI.</p>
