Hello! I'm **Kouyakou-Abalo SIMSOBA**, a **Google DeepMind Scholar** and holder of two Master's degrees — an **M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence for Science** (Stellenbosch University / AIMS South Africa) and an **M.Sc. in Mathematics, Statistics** (PAUSTI / JKUAT, as an **African Union Scholar**). I am originally from Togo and currently based in Lomé, where I teach at ESAM.

My research is at the intersection of **deep learning** and **medical image analysis**, with a focus on building models that are robust, well-calibrated, and trustworthy by design. My MSc thesis introduced **MultiMagNet**, a multi-magnification CNN fusion architecture for breast cancer histopathology — now submitted to **ICLR 2027**. My earlier work produced a novel **differentiable Minkowski–Log-Cosh loss function** for robust LSTM forecasting, published in **IEEE Access**. I am actively seeking a fully funded **Ph.D. position** in machine learning, statistics, or trustworthy AI.
