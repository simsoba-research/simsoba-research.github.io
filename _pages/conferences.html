  • Deep Learning Indaba 20262–7 Aug 2026
    Participant · Pan-Atlantic University, Lagos, Nigeria · Theme: Sovereign Intelligence. African Scale.

    The event. The Indaba is the largest annual gathering of the African machine learning community, founded in 2017 and now a registered charity run by volunteer organisers. The 2026 edition, its eighth, brought together more than 1,000 researchers, engineers, students and policymakers from about 45 countries. Its programme combines tutorials, research presentations, workshops, mentorship and dataset showcases, and attendance is by application. Google Research and Google DeepMind were among the sponsors.

    What I followed. Sessions on computer vision, trustworthy and ethical AI design, agentic AI, and AI evaluation in African contexts. The theme of building and evaluating AI systems that serve African realities is the same concern that drives my work on reliable medical AI.

  • MMED Clinic: Meaningful Modelling of Epidemiological Data15–26 Jun 2026
    Participant · ICI3D programme, SACEMA (Stellenbosch University) · AIMS South Africa, Muizenberg

    The event. MMED is a two-week clinic on data-driven, dynamical models of disease spread; the 2026 clinic was its 15th edition. Seminars, tutorials and individual mentoring prepare participants to complete a team modelling project. The International Clinics on Infectious Disease Dynamics and Data (ICI3D) have trained hundreds of researchers across sub-Saharan Africa.

    What I did. My team modelled Mpox transmission in South Kivu, DRC, fitting SIR, SEIR and stochastic SEIRV models to data, with R-based cleaning pipelines and a final presentation. It gave me hands-on experience with parameter estimation and with communicating model uncertainty to a mixed audience.
