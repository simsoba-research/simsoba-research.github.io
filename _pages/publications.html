  • MultiMagNet: Multi-Magnification Fusion with Availability Masking, Magnitude Dropout, and Feature Alignment Regularisation for Robust Breast Cancer Histopathology ClassificationUnder review · ICLR 2027
    Simsoba, K.-A. & Ezugwu, A. E.-S. · 2026

    Four-stream fusion of all BreakHis magnification levels through availability masking, magnitude dropout and attention-gated fusion. Reaches AUC 0.935 while staying robust to missing inputs.   • MultiMagNet: Multimodal Fusion of Histopathology Imaging and Structured Clinical Data for Breast Cancer Diagnosis and StagingIn preparation · 2026
    Simsoba, K.-A. et al.

    Extends the M.Sc. thesis toward multimodal learning: fusing histopathology images with structured clinical records, with diffusion models, VAEs and vision-language models for synthetic data augmentation under limited labelled data.

    In short: combining tissue images with patient records, and synthetic training data, to diagnose and stage breast cancer with less labelled data.

  • Decision-Aware Breast Cancer Classification: Learning Deferral Policies for Human-AI CollaborationTechnical report · 2026
    Simsoba, K.-A. · NeurIPS-format course project, AIMS South Africa · mentored by Dr. Umang Bhatt

    Formalises when a deep vision model should act autonomously and when it should defer to an expert. Confidence-based deferral raises accuracy from 71.25% to 90.0% at 41.25% coverage while cutting costs by 24%.

    In short: a rule for when an AI should decide alone and when it should hand the case to a human expert, so accuracy rises while costs fall.

  • A Hybrid Minkowski–Log-Cosh Loss Function for Robust LSTM-Based Time Series ForecastingPublished · IEEE Access 2025
    Simsoba, K.-A., Ngesa, O., & Mageto, T. · IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 187307–187319 · Q1, IF 3.9 · Scopus & Web of Science

    A differentiable loss combining Minkowski and log-cosh terms to balance sensitivity to typical errors with robustness to outliers. Validated on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data: MAPE falls from 20.96% to 18.23% (13% relative improvement) and about 117,000 mispredicted cases are avoided in outlier-contaminated scenarios. First author.

    In short: a training objective that makes forecasts more accurate and less thrown off by unusual data points, tested on real malaria records.
