A Hybrid Minkowski–Log-Cosh Loss Function for Robust LSTM-Based Time Series Forecasting

Simsoba, K.-A., Ngesa, O., & Mageto, T.

IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 187307–187319 · 2025 · Q1, IF 3.9 · Scopus & Web of Science indexed

Introduces a differentiable loss combining Minkowski and log-cosh terms to balance sensitivity to typical errors with robustness to outliers. Validated on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data — reduces MAPE from 20.96% to 18.23% (13% relative improvement) and cuts mispredicted cases by ~117,000 in outlier-contaminated scenarios. First author: conceived the problem, designed and implemented the loss function, ran all experiments, wrote the manuscript.

MultiMagNet: A Multi-Magnification Fusion Framework for Breast Cancer Histopathology Classification

Simsoba, K.-A.

MSc Thesis (Awarded with Distinction) · AIMS South Africa / Stellenbosch University · 2026

Four-stream ResNet-50 architecture with gated attention fusion, feature-alignment regularisation, and magnitude dropout. Evaluated on BreakHis and BACH. Model reliability assessed through ECE, reliability diagrams, temperature scaling, MC Dropout uncertainty decomposition (aleatoric / epistemic), and Mahalanobis-distance OOD detection. Supervised by Google DeepMind Scholar programme at AIMS South Africa.

Decision-Aware Breast Cancer Classification: Learning Deferral Policies for Human-AI Collaboration

Simsoba, K.-A.

Course Project (NeurIPS format) · Algorithms for Human-AI Collaboration · AIMS South Africa · 2026

Formalises when a deep vision model should act autonomously versus defer to a stronger expert under explicit deployment cost structure. Derives the optimal cost-ratio confidence threshold and compares against a learned routing policy. Confidence-based deferral raises system accuracy from 71.25% to 90.0% at 41.25% coverage while cutting deployment costs by 24%. Mentored by Dr. Umang Bhatt.
