  • A Hybrid Minkowski-Log-Cosh Loss Function for Robust LSTM-Based Time Series ForecastingPublished · IEEE Access 2025
    Simsoba, K.-A., Ngesa, O., & Mageto, T. · IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 187307 to 187319 · Q1, IF 3.9 · Scopus & Web of Science

    A training loss that makes LSTM forecasts more accurate and less thrown off by unusual data points. On 11 years of national malaria surveillance data, MAPE drops from 20.96% to 18.23% (13% better), and about 117,000 mispredicted cases are avoided when outliers are present. I designed the loss, ran all experiments and wrote the paper.
