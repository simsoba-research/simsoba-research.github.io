MultiMagNet: Multi-Magnification Fusion with Availability Masking, Magnitude Dropout, and Feature Alignment Regularisation for Robust Breast Cancer Histopathology Classification

Simsoba, K.-A. & Ezugwu, A. E.-S.

A Hybrid Minkowski–Log-Cosh Loss Function for Robust LSTM-Based Time Series Forecasting

Simsoba, K.-A., Ngesa, O., & Mageto, T.

IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 187307–187319 · 2025 · Q1, IF 3.9 · Scopus & Web of Science

Introduces a differentiable loss combining Minkowski and log-cosh terms to balance sensitivity to typical errors with robustness to outliers. Validated on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data — reduces MAPE from 20.96% to 18.23% (13% relative improvement) and cuts mispredicted cases by ~117,000 in outlier-contaminated scenarios. First author.

Decision-Aware Breast Cancer Classification: Learning Deferral Policies for Human-AI Collaboration

Simsoba, K.-A.

NeurIPS-format course project · AIMS South Africa · 2026 · Mentored by Dr. Umang Bhatt

Formalises when a deep vision model should act autonomously versus defer to a stronger expert under explicit deployment cost structure. Confidence-based deferral raises accuracy from 71.25% → 90.0% at 41.25% coverage while cutting costs 24%.
