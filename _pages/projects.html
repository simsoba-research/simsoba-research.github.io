Trustworthy ML & Medical AI
MultiMagNet — Multi-Magnification Fusion for Breast Cancer Histopathology
MSc Thesis · AIMS South Africa / Stellenbosch University · Jan–Jun 2026 · Ongoing extension
Four-stream ResNet-50 architecture with gated attention fusion, feature-alignment regularisation, and magnitude dropout, evaluated on BreakHis and BACH. Currently extending toward multimodal fusion (imaging + structured clinical data) and generative augmentation via diffusion and vision-language models for limited-label regimes. Manuscript in preparation.

Decision-Aware Breast Cancer Classification — Deferral Policies for Human-AI Collaboration
Course Project (NeurIPS format) · Algorithms for Human-AI Collaboration · AIMS South Africa · Mar–Apr 2026
Formalises when a deep vision model should act autonomously versus defer to a stronger expert under an explicit deployment cost structure. Derives the optimal cost-ratio confidence threshold and compares against a learned routing policy. Confidence-based deferral raised system accuracy from 71.25% to 90.0% at 41.25% model coverage while cutting deployment costs by 24%. Mentored by Dr. Umang Bhatt.

Robust Learning & Forecasting
Hybrid Minkowski–Log-Cosh Loss for Robust LSTM Forecasting
MSc Thesis · PAUSTI / JKUAT · 2023–2025 · Published in IEEE Access 2025
Combined Minkowski and log-cosh terms into a single differentiable loss function to balance sensitivity to typical errors with robustness to outliers. Validated on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data — reduces MAPE by 13% relative to MSE baseline and cuts mispredicted cases by ~117,000 in outlier-contaminated scenarios.
