Trustworthy ML & Medical AI
  • MultiMagNet: Multimodal Breast Cancer Diagnosis and StagingJun 2026 – present
    M.Sc. thesis extension · AIMS South Africa · Ongoing

    Extends MultiMagNet toward multimodal learning by fusing histopathology images with structured clinical records. Investigates diffusion models, VAEs and vision-language models for synthetic data augmentation and better generalisation when labelled data is scarce. Manuscript in preparation.

  • Decision-Aware Breast Cancer Classification: Learning Deferral Policies for Human-AI Collaboration10 Mar – 7 Apr 2026
    Course project · Algorithms for Human-AI Collaboration · AIMS South Africa · mentored by Dr. Umang Bhatt · NeurIPS paper format

    Formalises when a deep vision model should act on its own and when it should defer to a stronger expert model, under an explicit deployment cost structure. Derives the optimal cost-ratio confidence threshold and compares it with a learned routing policy.

    • Confidence-based deferral raised system accuracy from 71.25% to 90.0% at 41.25% model coverage.
    • Cut deployment costs by 24%.

  • MultiMagNet: Multi-Magnification Fusion for Breast Cancer HistopathologyJan – Jun 2026
    M.Sc. thesis · AIMS South Africa / Stellenbosch University · Awarded with Distinction · Under review at ICLR 2027

    A four-stream ResNet-50 architecture that fuses all BreakHis magnification levels through availability masking, magnitude dropout, feature-alignment regularisation and gated attention fusion; evaluated on BreakHis and BACH.

    • Reaches AUC 0.935 while staying robust to missing magnification inputs.
    • Reliability assessed with ECE, reliability diagrams, temperature scaling, MC Dropout uncertainty decomposition and Mahalanobis-distance OOD detection.

Robust Learning & Forecasting
  • Hybrid Minkowski–Log-Cosh Loss for Robust LSTM Forecasting2023 – 2025
    M.Sc. thesis · PAUSTI / JKUAT · Published in IEEE Access, 2025

    Combines Minkowski and log-cosh terms into a single differentiable loss that balances sensitivity to typical errors with robustness to outliers. Validated on 11 years of national malaria surveillance data and across further time-series benchmarks.

    • Reduced MAPE from 20.96% to 18.23%, a 13% relative improvement.
    • Cut mispredicted cases by about 117,000 in outlier-contaminated scenarios.
