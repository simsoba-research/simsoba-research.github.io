<div class="section-divider"><span>Profile</span></div>

Mathematics and artificial intelligence researcher holding two Master's degrees from AIMS South Africa and PAUSTI. First author of a peer-reviewed *IEEE Access* paper on robust LSTM-based time-series forecasting. MSc thesis at AIMS developed **MultiMagNet**, a multi-magnification fusion framework for breast cancer histopathology, with model reliability assessed through calibration diagnostics, uncertainty quantification, and OOD detection.

Research interests: **machine learning**, **trustworthy AI**, **model calibration and uncertainty quantification**, **robust deep learning** — applied to computer vision, medical image analysis, and time-series forecasting. **Google DeepMind Scholar** (top 40, Africa) and **African Union Scholar** (top 9, Mathematics/Statistics). Actively seeking a fully funded Ph.D. in ML, statistics, or trustworthy AI.
